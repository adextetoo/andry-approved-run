Andry · the flow-blockers, drawn

The thirteenscreens that let the run finish

Thirteen plates, built on the Andry design system with no rule added to it and nothing in the approved 171 touched. Each one closes a place where a journey stopped: a slide that was never made, a step a stepper claimed was done, an account number a screen promised on the next screen, a document four plates asked people to accept.

They are drawn at 402 × 874 on the same component set as the run, so they can be read beside it without translation. Where a plate had to invent a fact — a bank, a fee, a loss rate — it is consistent with the figures already published across the 171.

One defect surfaced while matching #11. Its KYC stepper puts the step labels inside .kyc__l, which the system defines as a 2px connector line. The text overflows that 2px box and collides with the heading beneath it — visible on the approved plate today. C-04 and C-05 set the same labels in a nowrap span instead, so the new work does not inherit the collision. Fixing #11 itself needs a stylesheet change rather than a plate edit, and nothing approved was touched here.

Risk disclosure
Version 2.4 · in force 01 Jul 2026 9 min read
In plain words
You can lose everything you pay. An uninsured animal that dies leaves its units worth nothing. This is not a remote possibility; it happened to 47 animals in the past 12 months.
Prices fall as well as rise. Cattle prices moved between −14% and +23% over the past two years.
You may wait to sell. Half of listings clear in 9 days. One in twenty takes more than 60.
Insurance is narrower than people assume. It pays for death by named causes, minus a 10% excess. It never pays for a fall in price.

This summary is not the agreement. Where the two differ, the numbered text below is what binds.

The animal can die

1

Across the herd, 2.1 animals in 100 were lost in the 12 months to 30 June 2026. The rate is not even: it was 1.4% for cattle, 3.8% for goats and 4.1% for sheep, and it rises in the rainy season.

Where the animal was insured and the cause is covered, the underwriter pays the last valued price less a 10% excess, and that money is divided between unit holders. Where it was not, or the cause is excluded, your units become worth nothing and no compensation is due from Andry.

The price can fall

2

A unit’s value follows the animal’s valuation, which follows weight, breed, season and the market at the nearest trading centre. Feed cost, fuel price and demand around Sallah and Christmas all move it.

Between July 2024 and June 2026 the median cattle unit moved between −14% and +23% from its purchase price. Past movement does not predict future movement.

You may not be able to sell when you want

3

Selling requires another owner to buy. Andry does not buy units back and does not guarantee a buyer. In the past year the median listing sold in 9 days, but 5% took more than 60 days and 1% did not sell at all before the animal reached maturity.

Do not buy units with money you will need at a fixed date.

Kept at andry.ng/legal. Every earlier version stays published, and we tell you 30 days before a change takes effect.
